# Nicholas Van-Orton

**Independent researcher · Founder of NVO987 – Culture Visuelle Moderne et Contemporaine Association**

Nicholas Van-Orton is an independent researcher working across modern and contemporary visual culture, with a particular focus on Sonia Delaunay and the role of colour as a generative principle in artistic practice.

## Research

The documented research and publication record presented here includes work concerning modern and contemporary visual culture, colour theory, artistic practice, interdisciplinary research, and the work of Sonia Delaunay.

The research record is connected to a range of scholarly, bibliographic, archival, identity, and digital research infrastructures.
