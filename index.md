Nicholas Van-Orton

Independent researcher · Founder of NVO987 Association

Nicholas Van-Orton independent researcher and founder of NVO987 – Culture Visuelle Moderne et Contemporaine Association, a Paris-based association founded in 2026.

His research and publication record includes work on modern and contemporary visual culture, with a particular focus on Sonia Delaunay and the generative role of colour in artistic practice.
